The short answer

Nothing up front, and no fee if there is no recovery

Injury and wrongful death matters are taken on contingency. You pay no retainer, and the fee is a percentage of what is recovered. If there is no recovery, there is no attorney fee.

The fee

What the percentage is, and when it changes

33 1/3%

If the claim resolves before a lawsuit is filed.

40%

Once suit is filed.

45%

If the case is appealed.

The percentage steps up because the work does. A claim settled on a demand package is a different undertaking from one tried to a verdict and defended on appeal.

Case costs are separate from the fee. Records, filing fees, deposition transcripts and expert witnesses are advanced by the firm and deducted from the recovery after the fee is calculated. The full terms are set out in writing before you sign anything, and they are also on the fees page.

What it means in practice

Where the interests line up, and where they do not

A contingency fee puts the firm's money on the same side as yours: we are paid out of a recovery, so a claim that recovers nothing costs you nothing and costs us the work.

It also means we will tell you when a claim is not worth bringing, and when it is small enough that you should keep all of it rather than share a third. A case review costs nothing and that answer is part of it.
