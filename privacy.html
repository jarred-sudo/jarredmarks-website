Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you fill in a form on this site, we receive what you type: your name, contact details, and the description of your matter. When you call the firm, our answering service records your name, number, and the reason for your call and sends it to us. We use this information to run a conflict check, respond to you, and evaluate whether we can help.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Analytics and the Google Ads tag. They set cookies or use device identifiers to measure how visitors find and use the site, and to tell us when a visit leads to a call or a form submission.

Online advertising

We advertise online. Third-party vendors, including Google, show our ads on sites across the internet. These vendors use cookies and device identifiers to serve ads based on a person's past visits to this website and other websites.

You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google at Google Ads Settings, and from many other vendors at the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

What we do not do

We do not sell the information you send us. We share it only with the service providers that help us operate the site and answer calls, and as the law requires.

Confidentiality

Contacting the firm through this site does not create an attorney-client relationship. Please do not send confidential details until the firm has confirmed it can represent you.
