Why This Firm

What a Utah Wrongful Death Claim Actually Requires

01

Whether You Need Probate, and When

Utah lets the heirs bring a wrongful death claim directly. A surviving spouse, adult child, or parent can also settle a policy-limits claim with an insurer 45 days after the death on a notarized affidavit, without opening an estate, up to $25,000 on liability and uninsured motorist coverage and $10,000 on underinsured. Above those caps, someone has to be appointed. We tell you which situation you are in before you spend anything.

02

We Handle the Appointment When It Is Needed

Where the claim is worth more than the affidavit route allows, or the insurer disputes it, the case is brought by a personal representative for the benefit of the heirs. Opening the estate and obtaining that appointment is probate work, and this firm already does it.

03

The Division Among Heirs Is Its Own Dispute

A recovery belongs to the heirs the statute names, and whoever brings the claim owes the others a fiduciary's duties. Families disagree about the split. Estate and trust litigation is a practice of this firm, not something handed off when it turns difficult.

04

Coverage Usually Sets the Ceiling

Many Utah policies are written at or near the state minimum. We identify every available layer of coverage early, because available insurance, rather than the severity of the injury, often decides what a claim can actually pay.

Common Questions

Utah Injury Claim FAQs

01

What does it cost to hire you?

Nothing up front. The fee is a percentage of what we recover, and there is no fee if we recover nothing. Case costs such as records, filing fees, and experts are separate from the fee and are explained in writing before you sign.

02

Should I take the insurer's first offer?

Not before you know what your treatment will end up costing. An early offer is made while the medical picture is incomplete, and accepting it closes the claim.

03

What if the other driver had no insurance?

The claim shifts to the uninsured or underinsured motorist coverage on your own policy. That is still a contested claim, and your insurer defends it like any other.

04

Who brings a wrongful death claim?

Under Utah law the heirs may bring it, or a personal representative may bring it for their benefit. A surviving spouse, adult child, or parent can also settle a limited policy-limits claim on a notarized affidavit 45 days after the death, without opening an estate. Which route fits depends on the size of the claim and whether the insurer disputes it.
