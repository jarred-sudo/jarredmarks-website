How We Help

Family Law Matters We Handle

Divorce, Contested & Uncontested

Representation from the first filing through the decree, whether you and your spouse agree on most terms or on none of them.

Child Custody & Parent-Time

Decision-making authority and the parenting schedule, negotiated where the parents can agree and litigated where they cannot.

Child Support

We set support in divorce and custody cases, and we litigate disputes over the income figures support is calculated from.

Alimony

Claims for alimony and defense against them, including disputes over need, earning capacity, and duration.

Property & Debt Division

We divide the marital home, other real estate, business interests, and retirement accounts, along with the debts attached to each.

Modification of a Decree

When your circumstances change, we ask the court to revise the custody or support terms of an existing decree. We also oppose modifications the other side files.

Paternity

We establish or contest parentage, then obtain the custody and support orders that follow from it.

Enforcement of Decrees & Orders

If your former spouse stops paying support, refuses to transfer property the decree awarded you, or ignores the parenting schedule, we ask the court to enforce its order.

How the Case Works

What to Expect From This Firm

01

A Divorce Is Litigation

Your case will have pleadings, an exchange of financial information, negotiation, and, if you and the other side cannot agree, a trial. We are a litigation firm, and we prepare a contested case on the assumption that a judge may decide it, because a spouse who sees that preparation has a reason to settle on reasonable terms.

02

A House, a Business, or a Retirement Account

When the marital estate includes real estate or a closely held business, the hard questions are who holds title and what the asset is worth. We answer those same questions for clients in our real estate and business litigation practice.

03

What to Bring to the Case Review

Bring any papers you have been served with or have filed, your last two tax returns, recent pay records, and your account statements. If you own a home, bring the deed and the mortgage statement. If a decree or custody order already exists, bring that too.

04

Billed Hourly Against a Retainer

You deposit a retainer into the firm's trust account, and the work is billed by the hour against it. When the balance falls below the threshold in your engagement letter, you restore it. The letter states your hourly rate and retainer before you sign.

Common Questions

Utah Divorce & Family Law FAQs

01

Do I need a lawyer if we already agree?

You may not need one for the whole case. Your agreement still has to be written into papers a judge will sign, in terms either of you can enforce later. We can review the agreement you reached or represent you from filing through the decree.

02

Do you handle custody-only cases?

Yes. We represent parents who were never married to each other, and parents asking the court to change or enforce an existing custody order. Where parentage has to be established first, we handle the paternity case as part of the same matter.

03

What does a divorce cost?

It depends on how much of the case is contested. Family law matters are billed hourly against a retainer, so a case that settles early costs less than one that goes to trial. Court filing fees and other costs are separate from the fee. Your rate and retainer are stated in writing before you sign.

04

Which counties do you serve?

We take family law cases in Salt Lake, Davis, Weber, and Utah counties. The firm is fully virtual, so meetings happen by phone or video and documents move by email.
